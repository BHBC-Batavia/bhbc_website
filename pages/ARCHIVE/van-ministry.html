Van Ministry

Free transportation to church for children and families in the Batavia area.

Free Transportation

Every Child Deserves a Chance to Hear the Gospel

We believe that no child should miss Sunday School and church simply because their family does not have a way to get there. Blessed Hope Baptist Church operates a 15-passenger van to pick up and drop off children and families throughout Batavia and the surrounding towns every Sunday morning.

The van ministry is open to people of all ages, but it is especially designed with children in mind. If you know a child who needs a ride to church, let us know and we would love to be able to accommodate.

Vehicle

15-Passenger Van

Services Covered

Sunday Morning, Sunday Evening & Wednesday

Area Served

Batavia & Surrounding Towns

Cost

Completely Free

Simple Process

How to Request a Ride

Getting a child or family to church is simple. Reach out ahead of time and we will take care of everything. Here is how it works.

1

Send Us an Email

Email us at info@blessedhopebaptist.church with the child's name, your address, and which services you need a ride for. We can set you up for Sunday School and the Worship Service, the Evening Service, Wednesday Bible Study, or any combination. Try to reach out at least a day ahead so we can plan the route.

2

We Will Confirm Your Pickup

Someone from our team will reply to confirm your pickup time and any details about your location. We will work around your schedule as best we can.

A permission form is required for anyone under 18. A paper form will be provided on your child's first pickup and must be signed before the ride begins.

3

We Come to Your Door

On Sunday morning the van will come right to your door. Children are greeted, picked up safely, and brought to church. When the service is over, we bring them home.

Common Questions

What People Ask

Is a permission form required?

A signed paper permission form is required for anyone under 18. We will bring the form on your child's first pickup. It must be completed and signed before the ride begins. It only needs to be done once.

Does my child have to attend Blessed Hope to use the van?

Not at all. The van is open to any child or family in the area, whether you have never visited before or you have been attending for years. If a child wants to come to church, we want to help make that happen.

Which services does the van run for?

The van is available for Sunday School and the Worship Service at 9:30 and 10:30 AM, the Sunday Evening Service at 5:30 PM, and Wednesday Bible Study at 7:00 PM. You can request a ride for any one of those services, or all of them. Just let us know what you need when you reach out.

How far out does the van travel?

We serve Batavia and the surrounding towns and cities in the area. If you are unsure whether your address is within our range, just email us and we will let you know.

What if I only need a ride one time?

That is completely fine. There is no commitment required. You can request a ride for a single Sunday or set up a regular pickup. Whatever works for you.

Know Someone Who Needs a Ride?

Whether it is a child in your neighborhood or a family without transportation, reach out and we would love to be able to accommodate. All ages are welcome.
